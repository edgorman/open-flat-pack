# 1. More ranges

v2 widens the catalogue from KALLAX to IKEA UK's other modular storage ranges. Everything else works as in [v1](../v1/0001-overview.md): the catalogue keeps facts only, is generated by `services/backend/scripts/scrape-catalogue` and is reviewed as a diff.

## Ranges

| Range | Parts | Rests on | Offers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BILLY bookcase | 40 and 80 cm wide, 106 and 202 cm high | `floor` | a `billy_front`; the 202 cm ones also a `billy_top` surface |
| BILLY height extension unit | 40 and 80 cm wide, 35 cm high | `billy_top` | a `billy_front` |
| OXBERG and HÖGBO doors | 40 cm wide, 35, 97 and 192 cm high | `billy_front` | - |

BILLY shelves, corner fittings, the drawer on castors and the bookcase with a foldable table fit none of these rules and are left out. A BILLY bookcase comes with its shelves.

Slot names are per range, `billy_top` rather than KALLAX's `top` and `billy_front` rather than a shared name, so that only a height extension stands on a BILLY and only BILLY's doors hang on it.

## Fronts

A third slot kind joins cell and surface:

- **front** - the face of a bookcase or cabinet, which doors cover. A part resting here must lie wholly inside it. Any number of parts may share a front, as long as they don't overlap each other, so an 80 cm BILLY takes two 40 cm doors side by side, or a 97 cm door below a gap of open shelves.

A part may overlap the part whose front it sits in, as it may the part whose cell it sits in.

Search lists doors as width by height only, so the scraper gives each a 20 mm depth for the preview, where a door is drawn on its bookcase's front face rather than behind it.

## Stacking

A design is stacked, and gets the "fix to the wall" note, when any part stands on a surface other than the floor or a `base`. A BILLY height extension counts.
